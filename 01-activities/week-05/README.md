### This Week

Collisions as a concept are something I have familiarity through video games analysis videos on youtube. What came to mind was coyote time, when a ground's contact point is extended beyond the visual to make it feel more fun to play. I wonder if I can apply that to pong and flappybird. 

I think the vectors are useful but math is not my strong point.
I understand the difference between speed and velocity, but it is quite tricky to imagine it through code.
the .mag() and .heading() feels the most foreign concept so far to me haha. 

I thought that activity 5a the bouncing balls could be treated as videogame entities, so I made the collision change to have their color flash red.

I can see boolean variables and states coming together. I supposed you can store a great amount of information and reactions with a single trigger. Learning about game logic, that is definely something I want to try playing with.

I'll be working with Jacee on the game project.
